PROFESSIONAL PROJECT· January 2025

Warhammer 40K

Contexte

Project constraints

Develop a Vertical Slice using Unreal Engine 5 in 4 weeks, providing a retro game interpretation of an existing IP.

The project asked for creative restrictions regarding set games and IPs, while keeping a comprehensible and cohesive gameplay experience, representative of the source material.

My role as Producer

Making sure planning, coordinating teams and production follow-up are carrying on, while accounting for technical constraints and deadlines.

Stressing out features, anticipating risks and amending plans were the main goals in order to deliver a functional Vertical Slice in the time allocated for the project.
